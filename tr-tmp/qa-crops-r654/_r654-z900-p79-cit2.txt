## (2) Blue label and number

Two blue bracketed labels appear, one per line:
- First line: **[سورة البقرة الآية : 30]**
- Second line: **[سورة البقرة الآية : 31]**

Note: the digits are printed in **Western/European numerals "30" and "31"**, not Arabic-Indic ٣٠/٣١ — so the "first digit ٣ vs ٣?" question doesn't apply; the first digit is a Latin **3** and the second is **0** / **1**. The sūra name is **البقرة**, not الحج. [؟] on the last digit of the second label: it reads 31, though the second glyph could arguably be scanned as ١ — it is a plain vertical stroke, i.e. "1".
